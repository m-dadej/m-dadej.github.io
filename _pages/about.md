I am **Head of Economic Research for the Central and Eastern Europe (CEE) region** at
[Coface](https://www.coface.com/), the French credit insurer, where I cover the economies of
Central and Eastern Europe and Central Asia. I am also a member of the board at the
[Association of Polish Economists](https://tep.org.pl/)
(<span lang="pl">Towarzystwo Ekonomistów Polskich</span>).

My first name is pronounced *Muh-TEH-woosh*.

## What I work on

My interest centres on the nexus of finance and macroeconomics, and on applied quantitative
methods in economics.

In my day-to-day work I follow **corporate insolvencies** across Central and Eastern Europe —
who is failing, in which sectors, and what that says about the wider economy — alongside
growth and trade dynamics, the effects of geopolitics on the region, and country-specific
developments from Poland and Czechia through to the Western Balkans and Central Asia. I
[comment on these topics regularly in the media](/in_media/), in both international and
regional outlets.

My academic work is on **systemic risk, financial contagion and business cycle transmission**.
You can find [my papers here](/publications/), the [talks I have given](/talks/) at Oxford,
Warsaw, Marseille and elsewhere, and a [CV](/cv/) with the longer version.

## Background

I completed my PhD at the [University of Brescia](https://www.unibs.it/en), with research
focused on quantitative methods for macro-financial stability. During my doctoral studies I
was a visiting researcher at the [University of Mannheim](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/). I
obtained my Master's degree in Economics at the [University of Gdańsk](https://ug.edu.pl/),
including an academic exchange at the [University of Pisa](https://www.unipi.it/index.php/english).

Before starting the PhD I was a quantitative analyst at an investment bank, developing stress
testing models. I have authored papers in computational economics and empirical finance, and I
write [open-source software](/code/) — including
[MarSwitching.jl](/publications/marswitching-jl-julia-markov-switching/), a Julia package for
Markov switching models.
