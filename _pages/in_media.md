As **Head of Economic Research for the Central and Eastern Europe (CEE) region** at Coface, I
regularly provide expert commentary to international and regional media on macroeconomics,
**corporate insolvencies**, the effect of geopolitics on growth, trade dynamics and
country-specific developments across the region.

Below is a curated, non-exhaustive selection of media appearances. Journalists working on
economic, political-economy or CEE-related stories are welcome to contact me for interviews,
quotes or background analysis.

**Email**: mateusz.dadej (at) coface.com
